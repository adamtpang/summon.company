Why it's different

A dashboard tells you the number. This tells you the move.

Reporting is everywhere. Decisions are the scarce thing. Every metric in vitals.run carries the one next action attached to it — so the dashboard isn't a rear-view mirror, it's a to-do list ranked by impact on three outcomes:

Outcome 01

Save time

The constraint is named and the next action is the one that moves it. No dashboards to interpret, no meetings to decide what's already obvious.

Outcome 02

Save money

Burn, runway, and expenses are first-class. You see the number that ends the company long before it does — and what to cut.

Outcome 03

Grow revenue

One north star, the weekly inputs that drive it, and the trend. What's working compounds; what isn't gets killed.

One OS, every company

Run a portfolio from first principles.

Start a company or partner on one — it gets a vitals dashboard that lives in its own repo. Same renderer, same shape, so every venture reads the same way. The codebase is the knowledge base; the data file is the state; your AI is the runtime that keeps them honest.
