About Summon

Updated August 31, 2026 · hello@summon.company

Summon is a provider-neutral control plane for AI-agent companies. It classifies the work, finds one binding constraint, assigns one accountable AI employee, and returns sensitive sends, spending, legal actions, and commitments to a human board for approval.

What Summon operates

Summon coordinates Engineering, Design, Marketing, Sales, Finance, Operations, Support, and Legal from one company context. Commercial work can use that eight-department formation, while research, personal tools, infrastructure, media, and archived projects keep the smaller operating shape that fits their actual beneficiary and evidence target.

How control stays with the board

Summon can run recurring work through supported AI executors, but configured budgets remain hard stops and high-risk actions return to the human board. Sending messages, spending money, accepting legal commitments, and acting in a person’s name require approval rather than being treated as routine automation.

Who operates this site

Summon and summon.company are operated by Anchor Marianas LLC. Product, privacy, billing, accessibility, and security questions can be sent to hello@summon.company. The current commercial terms are stated on the homepage and in the linked Terms and Privacy pages.

Where to verify the work

Summon publishes a changelog, machine-readable guidance in llms.txt, and public source and releases on GitHub. Those records are evidence of shipped software; they are not claims of customer revenue or guaranteed business outcomes.
