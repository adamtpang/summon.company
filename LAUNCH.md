# summon.company — launch and market plan

Positioning: **Claude is for codebases. Summon is for companies.** Hire AI employees for
$99/mo each; you're the board. Market it, never push it (offers as postable artifacts with
a buy link). Target strangers and businesses, never NS friends.

## The wedge (free to paid)
1. **Free diagnosis** at `/diagnose` (the front-door build): paste your business, get your
   stage, your one binding constraint, and a founder-precedent from the 48-episode corpus.
   This is the shareable, undeniable hook. It costs nothing and proves the IQ.
2. **"Summon the fix"**: the diagnosis ends with the formation that fixes it. First
   employee is one click.
3. **$99/mo per employee**, budget-capped. Add as many as the business needs.

## The moat, said out loud
- **A visible, real founder-CEO in a pseudonymous field.** Paperclip and the rest hide.
  You show your face and run your own company on Summon in public. That IS the marketing.
- **The diagnosis is grounded in 48 real founders** (Rockefeller, Jobs, Todd Graves...).
  Nobody else's advice traces to a corpus.
- **Company Zero**: Summon builds and runs Summon. The proof is the product.

## Channels (in priority order)
1. **Build in public on X**: daily posts of the agents working, vitals rising, a real
   number from the runtime logs (never a metric the logs can't prove, the 11x rule). The
   overnight-builder mornings (see the overnight system) are ready-made content.
2. **The free diagnosis as a lead magnet**: post "paste your business, get a free diagnosis
   from 48 founders" with the link. Every diagnosis is a shareable artifact.
3. **The leaderboard** (`summon.company/agents`, companiesmarketcap of the AI-agent
   economy) as an SEO + curiosity surface.
4. **Launch beats**: Product Hunt + Hacker News ("I run my company on a team of AI
   employees, and you can watch"), one strong Show HN with the live diagnosis.
5. **Direct proof**: idiguam.com (the family business) and one Guam SMB as public case
   studies of Summon improving a real business.

## First 100 users
Solo founders and tiny teams who feel the pain of "I can't afford to hire." Find them in
indie-hacker and build-in-public communities, not the NS. The free diagnosis converts the
curious; the $99 employee converts the ones who see their constraint named correctly.

## 14-day launch sequence
- Days 1-3: ship `/diagnose`, prove it on 5 real businesses, record the reactions.
- Days 4-6: build-in-public thread daily; post 3 free diagnoses of well-known startups.
- Day 7: Show HN + Product Hunt with the live diagnosis + "watch my company run itself."
- Days 8-11: onboard the first paying employee for 3 real founders; publish the vitals.
- Days 12-14: publish the idiguam case study; open the leaderboard.

## The one metric
Paying AI employees live (real Stripe subscriptions). Everything else is a leading
indicator of that number.
