# VIT-103 — Decision card: what we sell, to whom, at what price

Owner: Vitals CEO. Prepared 2026-07-16. Status: awaiting board ratification.
Rule: plain language. Board decides; on ratification the sentence goes into NORTH_STAR.

---

## THE DECISION CARD (read this, decide, skip the rest if you trust the work)

**We sell** a hireable AI employee that owns one real outcome end-to-end (board approves
every send and spend), fed by a free instant whole-company diagnosis, on top of a company
context file we never sell.

**To** solo, technical-ish founders — 1 to 5 real projects, revenue intent, zero staff —
reached self-serve in founder communities (lead lane). SMB owner-operators are the
higher-touch expansion lane, seeded by service engagements like Quantus, not the front door.

**At** $99/employee/mo, flat, no usage metering — but the *entry offer is one first
employee at $99*, not "staff the 8-person formation for $792." Land-and-expand grows the
employee count per company over time.

**One-line why:** $99 for one employee vs a $70K salary is an obvious yes and a small
first check; a free diagnosis earns the right to ask for it; the accumulating company
context makes leaving expensive. Selling the *whole formation* up front collides head-on
with a direct competitor (Cofounder.co) that sells an entire company of agents for $20–50/mo.

**What would change my mind:** real conversion data (once the first-$99 flow is live)
showing founders balk at per-employee pricing but convert at a flat whole-formation price
— then flip the unit to per-formation. Or ICP-1 self-serve failing to produce a repeatable
first dollar while service-led ICP-2 does — then lead with the service lane.

**Reversibility:** price and packaging are REVERSIBLE (change anytime, cheap). The
positioning — "AI *employees* you *hire*, the human is the *board*" — is STICKY; changing
it later resets brand recognition and the category we are trying to own. So: commit to the
positioning, hold pricing loosely.

## 1. Evidence pass — which ICP converts fastest?

**Honest baseline (11x rule): we have ZERO first-party conversion data.** Revenue is $0,
Stripe is not connected, no paying company exists. Any claim about "which ICP converts
fastest" cannot be proven from our own logs today. The market-cap snapshot confirms it:
4 companies on the plane, 0 paying, $99 anchor with 0 sold. So this pass is (a) the one
internal signal we do have, plus (b) what comparable products reveal.

- **Internal signal — the only real external engagement is Quantus, and it is ICP-2-shaped.**
  Quantus is a bespoke, Adam-brokered *service* relationship (ambassador/white-hat), not a
  self-serve $99 seat. It is evidence the higher-touch lane can start a relationship — but
  it is not a repeatable funnel and it cannot run without board-approved sends. It does not
  tell us self-serve conversion.

- **Landing / waitlist analytics: none exist yet.** There is no waitlist form in the repo
  (CTAs are Stripe / cal.com per the VIT-63 finding), so there is no intake funnel to read.
  This is itself a finding: we cannot measure ICP conversion because we have not shipped the
  instrument that would measure it.

- **Comparable products — what converts fastest in this category (external, dated):**
  | Product | What it sells | Entry price | Motion |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | **Cofounder.co** (General Intelligence Co.) — *direct competitor, same "run a whole company with AI" pitch* | Whole company of agents (eng/sales/mktg/design/finance/ops) | **$20/mo Pro, $50/mo Team + token/compute usage** | self-serve |
  | VenturOS | Named AI executive team | Free early access → flat monthly, no metering | self-serve |
  | Sintra | Multi-role AI "employees" | $97/mo (whole workspace, multiple roles) | self-serve |
  | Lindy | AI assistant (per user) | $49.99 → $99.99 → $199.99/mo, usage tiers | self-serve |
  | Devin (Cognition) | Coding agent | $20/mo Core + ACU consumption; $500 Team | self-serve → sales |
  | Artisan Ava | AI SDR (one job) | from $199/mo per seat + contacts | sales-assisted |
  | Beam AI | Enterprise process agents | $499+/mo, $10k+ setup | sales/enterprise |

  **Read:** the cheap, fast, self-serve, minutes-to-buy conversions in this category all
  happen at the *individual/technical-founder* end buying a *single* agent (Devin $20,
  Lindy $49.99, Cofounder $20). The higher-LTV buyers (Beam, Artisan) require a sales motion
  we cannot run yet (no board-approved outbound). This lines up with ICP-1 as the fastest
  *repeatable* first-dollar lane and ICP-2 as the higher-touch expansion lane.

**Conclusion:** lead acquisition with **ICP-1 (solo technical founder), self-serve, single
first employee.** It is the only lane where we can get a repeatable, no-board-send,
minutes-to-buy first-$99 datapoint — which is exactly the company's binding constraint
(# companies > 0). Keep ICP-2 as the service-wedge expansion lane, seeded by Quantus.

## 2. Kill-test the $99 anchor — is per-employee/mo the right unit?

**The case FOR per-employee/mo:**
- Anchors cleanly against a ~$70K human salary — "$99 vs $70,000" is the whole pitch.
- Matches the product's mental model: a *company* is made of *employees* you *hire*.
- Powers the market-cap model's #2 lever (land-and-expand, employees per company, NRR>120%)
  — every new employee is expansion revenue with no new logo cost.
- Our fuel doctrine (agents run on the founder's own subscription, ~$0 marginal cost) lets
  us offer FLAT pricing with NO usage metering — a differentiator VenturOS explicitly
  markets against Cofounder's token+compute surprise.

**The case AGAINST (the kill-test bites here):**
- A direct competitor with our exact pitch (Cofounder.co) sells the *whole company of
  agents for $20–50/mo.* An 8-employee formation at $99 = **$792/mo** — 15–40× the direct
  competitor's whole-company price. To a price-sensitive solo founder that is a losing
  first comparison.
- The failure isn't the *unit*, it's the *entry bundle.* "Free diagnosis → now staff all 8
  = $792/mo" is sticker shock that kills the exact conversion we most need.
- Per-seat pricing only feels fair when each seat does a visibly distinct job with its own
  output (Artisan = one SDR seat). Early on, a formation can feel like one product, which
  makes 8× seats feel like a tax, not 8 hires.

**Verdict — keep the unit, fix the bundle.** Retain $99/employee/mo as the anchor (it is
defensible and reversible), but make the *entry offer a single first employee at $99* — the
smallest true "hire," one visible outcome — and let land-and-expand add employees. Do NOT
sell the full formation at first touch. Against Cofounder's $20–50 do not compete on being
cheaper per agent; compete on **one accountable employee that owns a verified outcome, with
the human as board, at flat no-surprise pricing.** Revisit the unit itself only if live
conversion data shows founders prefer a flat whole-formation price.

## 3. The recommendation (restated as the card above)

- **X (product):** one hireable AI employee that owns an outcome end-to-end; free instant
  whole-company diagnosis as the lead magnet; accumulated company context as the never-sold
  moat.
- **Y (ICP):** LEAD = solo technical-ish founder (1–5 projects, revenue intent, zero staff),
  self-serve, founder communities. EXPANSION = SMB owner-operator, service-wedge, higher
  touch/LTV. Strangers only.
- **Z (price):** $99/employee/mo, flat, no metering; entry offer = one first employee at
  $99, not the $792 formation; land-and-expand grows seats per company.

**If the board ratifies, the NORTH_STAR sentence becomes:**
> We sell hireable AI employees ($99/employee/mo, flat — start with one, not the whole
> formation), fed by a free whole-company diagnosis, to solo technical founders first and
> SMB owner-operators as the higher-touch expansion lane; strangers only.

## 4. What this does NOT decide (kept out of scope on purpose)
- Outcome/metered pricing on top of the anchor (e.g. a sales employee earning on sales) —
  a later upside lever, not the entry decision.
- The waitlist/intake instrument itself — flagged as the missing measurement tool; belongs
  to a Marketing/Engineering follow-up so ICP conversion becomes measurable.
- No external sends were made. Research and packaging only, per the issue.
