Summon Desktop — proposed information architecture

VIT-51 · board review contact sheet · source: doc/research/DESKTOP-UX-SKELETONS.md · 2026-07-15

Claude Desktop and Codex converged on the same six-piece skeleton — Summon adopts it as table stakes and differentiates one level up: persistent employees with roles, a company-level decision queue, and board-grade reporting. The human is the board, not the driver.

8Adopted rules (anti-patterns)

No invisible autonomy — merge/publish/spend always lands in the Inbox first (Height 2.0 lesson).
Interrupt only when blocked on human input; badge or digest everything else (Slack tiers).
Bounded queue — show drain rate; suggest pausing hiring over threshold.
Never wait on the network — local-first console, no spinners (Linear bar).
No IDE cosplay — Summary transcripts default; terminals/diffs one level down.
