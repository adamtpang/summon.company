# First customers → hypergrowth: the playbook

> Sources: High Growth Handbook (Elad Gil, read 2026-07-19) + 2026 research on
> Cursor, Linear, Lovable, and founder-led-sales practice. Shared context for
> Echo (marketing) + Magnet (sales). Every claim carries its source.

## What the winners actually did (the pattern)

**Cursor** ($4M → $2B ARR in ~18 months): pure bottom-up PLG. A genuinely
useful free tier embedded in the daily workflow; individual developers
adopted, expensed it, and IT discovered the spend later — enterprise deals
STARTED from usage, not outreach. Logos (OpenAI, Shopify, Instacart) came
from their own engineers already using it.

**Linear**: quality-obsessed product + design-partner logos that begat logos —
OpenAI used it, then Ramp, then Cursor. Profitable two years in. The early
waitlist + craft reputation made the product itself the marketing.

**Lovable** ($75M in 24 months): consumer-grade accessibility ("vibe coding"
for everyone) then aggressively climbed to enterprise (Klarna, HubSpot).

**The founder-led-sales canon (2026)**: pick a narrowly-defined buyer; win the
first handful as DESIGN PARTNERS by hand; the milestone that matters is ~10
paying customers who resemble each other enough to reveal a repeatable
pattern. Build a "dream 20" list; warm intros first; first meetings are
problem discovery, not pitches; move fast to a live pilot. Hire the first
salesperson only AFTER the founder personally closed 10–20 and the motion
repeats (~$50k+ MRR).

**High Growth Handbook adds**: growth marketing (measurable, ROI-driven:
SEO/content, email, funnel) can carry a company alone — Wish hit $8B with
essentially no PR; press ≠ success. Tailor channels to where your buyers
actually live. PR accelerates recruiting/deals/fundraising, not first revenue.

## The Summon translation (our stage: $0 → first 10)

1. **The free wedge is live** — the Windows download + manual mode free.
   Cursor's lesson: the free tier must be genuinely useful ALONE. Every
   founder who runs one company on Summon free is a future paid seat.
2. **Founding cohort = design partners** — Anton (Regain pod, $392) and
   Michael are the first two of a dream-20. The trade is explicit: half price
   forever for weekly feedback + their story. Linear's lesson: the first
   logos are the marketing.
3. **Founder-led, market-not-push** — Adam sends; agents prep. Offers are
   postable artifacts with a buy link (announcements out, buyers in). The
   10-a-day discipline: ten high-intent prospects qualified daily by Magnet,
   trigger-based (posting about ops drowning, scaling agencies, new funding).
4. **Growth marketing over PR** (HGH doctrine): Echo's lanes — SEO/llms.txt/
   registries (live), programmatic content, build-in-public — compound;
   press releases don't. Revisit PR only when recruiting/fundraising needs it.
5. **The repeatability gate**: at 10 similar paying customers, write down the
   pattern (who, channel, objection, close) — THAT document is the hiring
   spec for any future sales seat, human or AI.

## Anti-patterns (do not)

- Cold outbound before the free wedge has users to point at.
- Selling to NS friends — beta testers, never prospects (standing rule).
- PR chasing before revenue (press ≠ success — HGH).
- Hiring/spawning sales capacity before the founder-closed pattern exists.
