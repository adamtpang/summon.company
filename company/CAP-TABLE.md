## Doctrine for future human hires (High Growth Handbook, Elad Gil)

- Hire for the next 12–18 months, not eternity — titles and scope will change
  every 6–12 months in hypergrowth; pragmatism over org perfection.
- Comp negotiation is a culture screen: HOW someone negotiates (what they
  optimize for — title, equity, growth) tells you who they are. Watch it.
- Never compromise on culture to close a hire; bad culture compounds as pain.
- Standard early-stage equity bands (to refine with counsel at first hire):
  advisor 0.1–0.5% (2-yr vest), first engineer/designer 0.5–2%, founding-team
  lateral 1–5% — always 4-yr vest, 1-yr cliff, board-approved.
- Every grant lands in this file the day it exists. The org page mirrors it.
