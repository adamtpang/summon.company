# $100M Leads — Alex Hormozi

> Principle: you are one advertising problem away; do the core four consistently, at ten times the volume that feels reasonable.

## The frameworks (in application order)

1. **Engaged leads are the only leads.** A lead is someone you can contact; an engaged lead has shown interest. All advertising exists to turn contacts into engaged leads for an offer worth wanting (the $100M Offers prerequisite).

2. **Lead magnets before lead asks.** Give away a complete solution to one narrow problem, free. Solving the narrow problem reveals the bigger problem your paid offer solves. Best magnets are cheap to deliver, high perceived value, and consumed quickly (tools, templates, diagnostics, first results). Summon's /diagnose page is exactly this pattern.

3. **The Core Four** (the only four ways one person can advertise): warm outreach (one-to-one, people who know you), posting free content (one-to-many, people who know you), cold outreach (one-to-one, strangers), paid ads (one-to-many, strangers). Do them yourself before delegating; most founders fail by doing too little of one rather than picking wrong.

4. **Rule of 100.** One hundred primary actions per day, every day, for one hundred days: 100 warm messages, or 100 minutes of content, or 100 cold contacts, or 100 dollars of ad spend. Volume is the strategy; consistency is the multiplier.

5. **Scale with More, Better, New, in that order.** More of what works until it breaks, then fix the constraint (better), and only then add a new platform or placement. Adding "new" first is how pipelines die.

6. **The four lead getters** multiply beyond your own hours: customers (engineered referrals: exceed promises, then ask), employees (they run the core four for you), agencies (hire to LEARN the channel, then bring it in-house), and affiliates (other businesses with your audience advertise for you).

7. **Open to goal.** Advertise until the math says stop: know cost to acquire a customer and lifetime gross profit; while payback stays inside your cash window, keep spending and increase volume.

## How Summon applies it

- The Marketing department's playbook IS the core four, sequenced: warm outreach and daily build-in-public content first (free, immediate), cold outreach once the offer has receipts, paid ads last.
- Every diagnose result whose constraint is distribution should prescribe core-four moves with Rule-of-100 volume, not vague "do marketing".
- Lead magnet ladder for Summon itself: /diagnose (free tool) reveals the execution gap; the founding seat is the paid offer behind it.
