# summon.company dream team

One company, eight operating departments, and one accountable owner per assignment.
Product strategy stays with the CEO; Product execution stays with Engineering.

| Seat | Archetype | Summon ownership |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Board / CEO | Adam | Mission, irreversible decisions, product direction, final gates |
| Cofounder | Codex, provider-neutral seat | Diagnose the binding constraint, marshal the roster, verify impact |
| Engineering | Elon Musk | Product execution, runtime, infrastructure, reliability |
| Design | Jony Ive | Product experience, identity, accessibility, visual proof |
| Marketing | Alex Hormozi | Positioning, demand, content, public proof |
| Sales | Ryan Serhant | Pipeline, prospecting, conversion, revenue evidence |
| Finance | John D. Rockefeller | Budget, pricing, cash discipline, runway |
| Operations | Jeff Bezos | Cadence, standards, workspaces, import and evidence systems |
| Support | Tony Hsieh | Onboarding, retention, issue resolution, customer evidence |
| Legal | Board-gated specialist bench | Contracts, privacy, compliance, incorporation, banking |

Do not create a ninth Product department. Do not dispatch multiple owners to the
same constraint. Swap Claude, Codex, or another provider only while the company has
zero queued or running agent runs.
