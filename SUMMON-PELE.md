# Summon to Pele: business-home contract

Owner clarification, 2026-09-09: Pele is Adam's home for businesses that summon.company creates.

## Roles

Summon creates and operates businesses through its company-building platform. Pele is the public home where Adam presents those businesses and the operating philosophy behind them. The Dojo remains the indie-hacker community. The elemental family remains Pele red/fire/business, Pangaea green/earth/philosophy, and Pacifica blue/water/music.

This relationship does not establish legal ownership, incorporation, a holding-company structure, or a claim that any particular business has already been created through Summon.

## Concrete repository boundary

Summon owns company creation and operational evidence. Existing evidence anchors are server/src/services/summon-application-template.ts and server/src/services/aether-portfolio.ts. Pele owns the public presentation in src/pages/index.astro and public/llms.txt.

When a business is ready to be represented, prepare a reviewed public record with its name, public URL, plain description, current status, and evidence of its Summon origin. Adam decides which businesses appear. Do not publish internal company records, private metrics, source credentials, customer information, or speculative businesses.

The initial Pele links to Summon and The Dojo describe the building platform and community. They are not a verified list of Summon-created businesses. No business registry, data feed, automatic company creation, or automatic publication is implemented by this connection.

## Coordination

Each repository declares the other as kin because Summon's creation and operations feed Pele's public business-home role. Both keep this contract. Manual read-only audit recipes can check the boundary and return evidence or proposed corrections. They do not authorize edits, runtime actions, messages to people, or publication. No exchange is dispatched merely by connecting the repositories.
