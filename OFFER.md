## One-liner

For **the solo founder or 1 to 3 person team running several projects at once**
who struggle with **being the only person in every department, so the work that
is not today's fire never happens**, **Summon** is a **control plane that staffs
your company with AI employees** that **diagnoses your binding constraint,
assigns one accountable employee to it, and does the work 24/7 while you approve
as the board**. Unlike **buying one autonomous AI worker for hundreds a month**,
we **staff all eight departments for $500 and run on the Claude or Codex
subscription you already pay for, so there is no API bill**.

## Buyer

- Solo founder, or a team of 1 to 3, running more than one project
- Technical enough to already have a Claude or Codex subscription
- Revenue intent, no staff, no budget for a hire
- Feels the pain as "I am the bottleneck in seven departments at once"
- Strangers only, never friends or Network School members

## Pain

They have more projects than hours. Marketing does not happen, sales does not
happen, support does not happen, because the founder is the only employee and
the only one who can do any of it. Hiring a human is thousands a month and weeks
of onboarding. Buying a single autonomous AI worker helps in exactly one
department and still costs hundreds a month, so staffing the whole company that
way is out of reach.

## Cure

One seat that staffs the whole org:

1. **Diagnose.** Paste the business. Summon finds its stage and its one binding
   constraint, theory-of-constraints style, before anything is bought.
2. **Summon.** Eight departments, each an AI employee: Engineering, Design,
   Marketing, Sales, Finance, Operations, Support, Legal.
3. **They work.** 24/7 on a heartbeat, budget-capped, executing through Claude,
   Codex, or whichever adapter fits, on the subscription the founder already
   pays for.
4. **You approve.** Anything touching money, law, or their name waits for a yes.
   Human-in-the-loop is the product, not a disclaimer.

## Alternative

- **Do it all themselves.** The status quo, and the real competitor.
- **One autonomous AI worker.** Solves one department, at a per-seat price that
  does not scale to eight. NOTE: the "$600/mo for ONE AI employee" comparison in
  the brief is not sourced anywhere in this repo. Verify a current competitor
  price before that number goes into public copy.
- **Hire a human.** Thousands a month and weeks of onboarding, for one
  department.
- **A pile of chat tools.** They hand back a draft and stop. Nobody owns an
  outcome, and nothing runs while the founder sleeps.
